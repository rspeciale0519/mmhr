# MMHR design concepts — five airy directions

Static HTML previews. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. No build step, no app code changes.

One shared brief: high-end, soft-technology software. Light backgrounds, whitespace, refined sans-serif type, restrained accents, rounded geometry, calm hover transitions. No metaphor themes.

Content follows the PRD customer journey: free buyer intake, a masked comparison of every published option, up to five RFP contenders, advisor disclosure, then an approved request. Sample companies, vendors, scores, and costs are fictional.

Each concept includes four screens: **Landing**, **Needs**, **Compare**, **Workspace**.

Proof screenshots: [`screenshots/`](screenshots/) at **1440px** and **390px**.

## Concept 1 — Haze

Cool mist and steel blue. Flat surfaces, hairline rules, a floating pill nav, a comparison strip beside the hero. Quiet and precise.

- **Palette:** Ice `#F5F8FB`, panel `#FFFFFF`, ink `#1A2433`, accent `#3E6F8A`, mist `#E3ECF3`
- **Fonts:** Sora + Source Sans 3
- **Surface:** Flat. No blur, no glow.
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-1-haze/landing.html) · [needs](concept-1-haze/needs.html) · [compare](concept-1-haze/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-1-haze/workspace.html)

## Concept 2 — Pearl

Warm pearl and sand with a muted terracotta accent. Oversized type, an uneven bento, large radii and a soft ambient glow.

- **Palette:** Pearl `#FBF6EF`, panel `#FFFAF5`, ink `#2C2620`, accent `#B56A4A`, sand `#F3E6D6`
- **Fonts:** Plus Jakarta Sans + Figtree
- **Surface:** Soft depth. Warm blobs, generous shadow, 24–32px radius.
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-2-pearl/landing.html) · [needs](concept-2-pearl/needs.html) · [compare](concept-2-pearl/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-2-pearl/workspace.html)

## Concept 3 — Still

Sage and porcelain. Frosted glass panels and a persistent app rail on every screen, including marketing.

- **Palette:** Sage ground `#F3F6F3`, porcelain `#FAFCFB`, ink `#1C2A24`, accent `#4F7A64`, leaf `#E4EDE6`
- **Fonts:** Be Vietnam Pro + Nunito Sans
- **Surface:** Glass. Backdrop blur, 1px translucent edges.
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-3-still/landing.html) · [needs](concept-3-still/needs.html) · [compare](concept-3-still/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-3-still/workspace.html)

## Concept 4 — Veil

Lavender-gray, not a purple gradient. Full-bleed horizontal chapters, a sticky frosted header, a pale wash behind the type.

- **Palette:** Lilac-gray `#F6F5F8`, panel `#FFFFFF`, ink `#24232B`, accent `#5E6A88`, wash `#EAE8F0`
- **Fonts:** Urbanist + Outfit
- **Surface:** Soft wash. Light linear gradient, medium radius, airy bands.
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-4-veil/landing.html) · [needs](concept-4-veil/needs.html) · [compare](concept-4-veil/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-4-veil/workspace.html)

## Concept 5 — Gleam

Near-monochrome with one champagne accent. Narrow copy measure, one glass product window, otherwise flat and spare.

- **Palette:** White `#FAFAFA`, panel `#FFFFFF`, ink `#171717`, accent `#9A7B4F`, line `#E7E7E7`
- **Fonts:** Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Sans
- **Surface:** Mostly flat. One blurred window as the product.
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-5-gleam/landing.html) · [needs](concept-5-gleam/needs.html) · [compare](concept-5-gleam/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-5-gleam/workspace.html)
