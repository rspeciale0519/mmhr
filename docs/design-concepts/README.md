# MMHR design concepts — five fresh directions

Static HTML previews for comparing visual directions. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a browser. No build step, no app code changes, no new packages.

Content follows the PRD customer journey: free buyer intake, a masked comparison of every published option, up to five RFP contenders, advisor disclosure, then an approved request. Buyers never pay MMHR; providers pay a referral fee; fees never rank vendors. Sample companies, vendors, scores, and costs are fictional. They are not product claims.

Each concept includes four screens:

- **Landing** — public marketing
- **Needs** — buyer intake questionnaire
- **Compare** — masked comparison matrix
- **Workspace** — logged-in customer case

Proof screenshots live in [`screenshots/`](screenshots/) and were captured at **1440px** (desktop) and **390px** (iPhone).

## Concept 1 — Docket

Records office. The selection is a case file: a vertical docket rail, manila folder tabs, exhibit stamps, and numbered process steps. Personality is clerkish and accountable, not a SaaS landing page.

- **Palette:** Manila `#FAF6F0` / `#EFE3CC`, ink `#1C1916`, docket red `#9B1B1B`, binder green `#1F4D3A`
- **Fonts:** Cormorant Garamond (display), Karla (body), IBM Plex Mono (file marks)
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-1-docket/landing.html) · [needs](concept-1-docket/needs.html) · [compare](concept-1-docket/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-1-docket/workspace.html)

## Concept 2 — Wayfind

Transit operations. Stages read as departures on a sodium-lit board. Condensed signage type, enamel on navy, live/hold status. Personality is operational and directional.

- **Palette:** Night navy `#0B1C2C`, sodium amber `#F5B942`, enamel `#F4F1EA`, signal green `#2FA36B`
- **Fonts:** Barlow Condensed (display), Barlow (body)
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-2-wayfind/landing.html) · [needs](concept-2-wayfind/needs.html) · [compare](concept-2-wayfind/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-2-wayfind/workspace.html)

## Concept 3 — Kiln

Ceramic studio. Glazed tiles, biscuit ground, a human advisor voice. White-glove without a private-club interior. Personality is warm, handmade, and calm.

- **Palette:** Biscuit `#F3E6D8`, clay `#C9784A`, soot `#2A221C`, glaze `#3F6F62`, celadon `#7BA89A`
- **Fonts:** Fraunces (display), Outfit (body)
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-3-kiln/landing.html) · [needs](concept-3-kiln/needs.html) · [compare](concept-3-kiln/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-3-kiln/workspace.html)

## Concept 4 — Cut

Editorial magazine. Crop marks, process color, and a yellow redaction bar over vendor names until disclosure. Personality is sharp and contemporary, with the matrix as the story.

- **Palette:** Newsprint `#EDE8DE`, carbon `#111111`, magenta `#E11D48`, cyan `#0077B6`, crop yellow `#F4D35E`
- **Fonts:** Syne (display), Source Serif 4 (body)
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-4-cut/landing.html) · [needs](concept-4-cut/needs.html) · [compare](concept-4-cut/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-4-cut/workspace.html)

## Concept 5 — Survey

Cadastral map. The market is a plat: forest border, coordinate grid, orange survey flags, parcels instead of vendor logos. Personality is measured and outdoor, like a field book.

- **Palette:** Cream `#F4EFE4`, forest `#1B4332`, survey orange `#E85D04`, water `#4A7C8C`
- **Fonts:** League Spartan (display), Libre Franklin (body)
- **Screens:** [landing](concept-5-survey/landing.html) · [needs](concept-5-survey/needs.html) · [compare](concept-5-survey/compare.html) · [workspace](concept-5-survey/workspace.html)
