How it works

01

Tell us what you need

Capture company context, current systems, requirements, compliance, budget, timeline, stakeholders, and decision process. Save and return anytime.

02

Compare every fit

See every approved option in one matrix. Names stay masked so the evidence decides, not the brand. Choose up to five RFP contenders.

03

Talk it through

An MMHR advisor reviews your requirements with you, then records disclosure of the names you chose.

04

Send one request

MMHR approves and sends a single RFP. Vendors answer in a structured portal. You see only approved replies.

05

Choose with confidence

Compare demos and quotes, pick a provider, and sign a direct contract with them. MMHR does not negotiate the legal contract.

Customers never pay

Software providers pay MMHR a referral fee. Buyers pay nothing for this help.

Fees never rank vendors

Referral fees stay outside every score. Rankings use approved evidence only.

Evidence before names

You compare capabilities first. Names appear after you review requirements with an advisor, before any RFP is sent.
