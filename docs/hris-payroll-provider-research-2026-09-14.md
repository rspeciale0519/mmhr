# HRIS with payroll: 50-provider research list

**Research date:** September 14, 2026  
**Scope:** US employers first, with global and regional options included  
**Status:** Internal research. Vendor approval and product demonstrations remain pending.  
**Purpose:** Support MMHR vendor catalog research and the approved Comparison matrix.

## How to use this document

This is a selected list of 50 providers with HR records and a documented payroll option. Numbers identify entries; they are not rankings.
Selection considers product breadth, US relevance, international coverage, and different employer needs. No common market-share dataset establishes these as the largest 50 providers.

The feature lists summarize publicly documented capabilities found during this research. They do not claim every configuration, regional variation, connector, or future feature.
Features can require separate modules, higher plans, services, or partner contracts. A listed capability does not mean that the base subscription includes it.
An omitted feature means **not established in this inventory**, rather than **not available**.

Sources are official product pages, feature lists, and help documentation. All linked sources were reviewed through web search or page access on the research date.
These sources describe vendor claims. They do not constitute independent product tests or MMHR approval.
Where a page redirects, the entry uses the current product name when the destination establishes that name.

**Payroll types:**

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Integrated | Payroll processing is offered within the named product or provider suite. Separate purchase can still apply. |
| Connected | A separate payroll engine or provider receives HR data through an integration. |
| Preparation | The HRIS collects, checks, or exports payroll inputs. Another system performs payroll processing. |
| Configurable | Software calculates payroll, but local rules, filings, payments, and maintenance need separate validation. |
| Managed | The provider offers payroll operations as a service. This does not automatically mean co-employment. |
| PEO / EOR | A professional employer organization or employer of record adds an employment-service arrangement. This differs from ordinary payroll software. |

Global HR coverage does not establish global payroll coverage. Payroll, tax filing, fund movement, and EOR availability can have different country lists.
The market column describes the researched offering. It does not certify support for every state, territory, worker type, or collective agreement.
